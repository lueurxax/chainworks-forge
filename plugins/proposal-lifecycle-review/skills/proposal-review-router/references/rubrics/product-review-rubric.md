# Product Review Rubric

Use only when the user asks for product review or the proposal depends on metrics, prioritization, experiment design, launch sequencing, adoption, or decision checkpoints.

## Focus areas

- Problem framing: target user, pain, current workaround, and urgency are concrete.
- Value: proposed outcome is materially better than current state.
- Scope: MVP, non-goals, sequencing, and tradeoffs are sharp.
- Metrics: leading metric, guardrail metric, and decision checkpoint are meaningful and measurable.
- Experiment/rollout: adoption path, hold criteria, and stop/continue decisions are explicit.
- Trust/business risk: privacy, money, support, operational, and brand risks are addressed.

## Sharp heuristics

- Do not invent metrics when the proposal is purely technical and product is not selected.
- Treat vanity metrics as incomplete when they do not drive a decision.
- Treat a missing decision checkpoint as a launch-risk finding when metrics are central.

## Finding requirements

Each finding must include evidence IDs, user or business consequence, required fix, acceptance criteria, leading metric, guardrail metric, decision checkpoint, and confidence.
