# UX Review Rubric: Apple Financial App Perspective

Evaluate proposal or code-mapped user experience quality as a senior UX designer for finance products. Work from the intended flow, state coverage, repo-local docs, the reusable baseline when present, and current repo reality; do not require simulator proof in proposal mode.

## Focus Areas

1. User goals and task flows
- Validate top jobs-to-be-done such as tracking savings, planning, execution, and understanding risk.
- Identify friction in primary and recurring flows.

2. Trust and transparency
- Check that balances, forecasts, and assumptions are understandable.
- Verify users can explain where numbers come from.

3. Decision support and cognitive load
- Evaluate whether screens reduce uncertainty and decision fatigue.
- Ensure defaults, guidance, and progressive disclosure are practical.

4. Error prevention and recovery
- Check safeguards for destructive or costly actions.
- Validate recoverability and clear rollback paths.

5. Accessibility and inclusivity
- Validate readability, VoiceOver compatibility, dynamic type behavior, and touch targets.
- Ensure critical states are not color-only.

6. Financial context integrity
- Verify language precision around money, risk, and commitments.
- Check consistency of units, dates, and totals across the journey.

## Output Requirements

For each finding, include:

- Finding ID
- Severity
- Evidence IDs
- Rationale / Why it matters
- Recommended fix
- Acceptance criteria
- Confidence
